Terms & Conditions & Participant Consent

Please review and accept before uploading your screenshots.

1. Research Purpose: This study investigates how user-provided "Smart Notes" help classify daily financial transactions into accurate spending categories compared to raw statement text alone. It is an independent research tool.

2. What We Record (Categorization Data Only): The intended study fields are amount, transaction type, reviewed merchant/context, privacy-reviewed remarks, coarse date/time, participant response and Smart Note. Review and remove identifiers before confirming.

3. What We DO NOT Record (Strict Privacy Protection): To protect your financial privacy, OCR sends the uploaded image to Google Gemini for extraction. The researcher export is designed to exclude Wallet Ref, Transaction No., DuitNow Ref and raw screenshot images. Google may process uploaded images under its applicable service terms. Automated masking is imperfect; review all merchant names, remarks and notes for personal information before saving. This standalone prototype still needs an approved, secure study backend and collection approval.

4. Participant Control: You can inspect and edit all extracted text using the "Edit" button or remove any screenshot (`X`) before submitting.
